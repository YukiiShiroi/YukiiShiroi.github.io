Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Camino collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only in your browser on this device. You can export them as a backup file or delete them at any time (Settings → Backup → Reset all progress).

Optional sync

If you turn on sync, your progress is saved to a secret Gist (a private file) on your own GitHub account, using a token you create. The token stays in your browser and is only sent to GitHub. Camino's maker never sees your progress or your token. You can revoke the token at any time in your GitHub settings.
